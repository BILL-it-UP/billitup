Not a POS

No barcode scanners, no thermal receipts, no shelf inventory. One clean paper size — A4 — built for documents that get emailed and filed, not printed at a counter.

GST-ready

GSTIN, HSN/SAC, and tax breakdowns where a corporate invoice needs them — plus PO/reference numbers and payment terms your clients' finance teams expect.

Yours to run

Self-host it on your own server with Docker Compose. No monthly fee, no vendor holding your client list — the code is on GitHub under MIT.

What's on the invoice

Everything a corporate billing cycle needs, line by line.

Every feature below ships in the open-source core — nothing held back for a paid tier.

01

Branded A4 invoices

Logo, address, bank/UPI details, signature and your standard terms — printed or emailed, with a proper "amount in words" line.

02

Quotes → Invoices

Send an estimate, and once it's accepted, convert it to an invoice in one click — same line items, new sequence number.

03

Credit notes

Issue a refund or correction against an invoice and its balance updates automatically — no manual reconciliation.

04

Recurring invoices

Set a retainer once — weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly — and BillItUp raises the next invoice on schedule, on its own.

05

Client-facing links

Share a read-only link to one invoice — your client can view and download the PDF without ever needing a login.

06

Email, from your own address

Invoices go out through your own SMTP account — a Gmail app password, or whatever you already use. Never a shared relay.

Why BillItUp

Most billing software is built for the shop counter.

Vyapar, and tools like it, are excellent for retail — thermal receipts, stock counts, a till. BillItUp is built for the other half of Indian business: agencies, consultants, and small companies who send a proper A4 invoice and get paid by bank transfer.
